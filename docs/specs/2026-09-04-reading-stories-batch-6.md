# Čitanje — story library, batch 6 (levels 2, 3 and 4)

14 stories: 4 at level 2, 4 at level 3, 6 at level 4. Completes level 4 at 40/40 and brings the
level 2–3 seed set to 20.

**On the level 2 stories:** they are deliberately thin. Under the level-2 constraint — every syllable
CV or CVC, no clusters, max two syllables — Croatian offers perhaps forty usable nouns and thirty
usable verbs. There is no room for plot. That is precisely why the spec hands levels 2–3 to
`storyTemplates.ts`: these 10 exist to seed the generator's vocabulary and prove the format, not to
carry a kid for a month.
